# Rallii Global Route Backlog

## Purpose and boundary

This is an editorial research backlog, not a live directory. A candidate does not appear in Rallii until it passes the published-route quality gate below.

- **Verified published catalogue:** 31 routes.
- **Researching now:** routes #32–35 (Madaraka Express, Reunification Express, East Rift Valley Railway, and Kuranda Scenic Railway).
- **Capacity today:** 219 published-route slots remain.
- **Expected capacity after #32–35:** 215 slots remain, for a 35-route catalogue.
- **Current editorial backlog:** 90 candidates. The backlog may exceed the publication cap because candidates compete for a limited number of fully verified places.

The cap of 250 is a quality ceiling, not a quota. A route may be deferred, replaced, or never published when its geometry, image licence, operating status, or editorial value is not strong enough.

## Status key

- **Research-ready** — strong route identity and likely primary sources identified; start here.
- **Needs geometry research** — a compelling route, but continuous railway geometry needs preparation and review.
- **Needs licensed photography** — route research is promising; image provenance is the primary gap.
- **Needs operator/source verification** — route identity, current operation, or practical details need primary-source confirmation.
- **Deferred** — retain as a future possibility; do not schedule production research yet.

## Route quality gate

A candidate may move from this backlog to a published Rallii route only after it has:

1. continuous, reviewed, correctly oriented prepared geometry from credible railway or mapped infrastructure sources;
2. stable route identity separated from changeable schedules, fares, reservation conditions, service patterns, weather, and closures;
3. current primary operator, infrastructure, tourism, or land-manager sources appropriate to the route;
4. at least one locally stored image with complete creator, licence, source, location, and access-date attribution;
5. representative stations, landmarks, Scenic Moments, direction-aware Best Side guidance, and practical information that are sourced or explicitly cautious;
6. generic Search, Discover, Compare, Plan, Stays, Saved, Rail Map, collection, relationship, and Guide integration;
7. tests for route identity, geometry continuity/orientation, search, timeline direction, Best Side behavior, relationships, collections, Compare, and personal library compatibility; and
8. TypeScript, lint, tests, production build, and mobile verification before publication.

Never use a candidate list as evidence that a route is operating, bookable, or suitable for publication. Every candidate requires fresh source review when it enters production research.
