# App Review response

Thank you for the opportunity to clarify Rallii’s business model. We have uploaded version 1.2, build 17, which removes the previous references to planned Rallii Pro functionality. Rallii Pro is not offered in this release. All available app functionality is free.

1. Who are the users that will use the paid content, subscriptions, features, and services in the app?

There are no users of paid digital content, subscriptions, or paid app features because Rallii does not offer them in this release. Rallii is intended for people exploring rail journeys, golf courses, hiking trails, mountain biking destinations, and snow destinations. All users can access the available guides, maps, supported scenic alerts, saved places, trip collections, and notes without payment or a subscription.

2. Where can users purchase the content, subscriptions, features, and services that can be accessed in the app?

There is no purchase mechanism for Rallii digital content or app features, either inside the app or on an external website. Rallii has no subscription checkout, paywall, or paid upgrade. The app includes external links to third-party travel bookings, such as accommodation, flights, activities, and car hire, and to physical travel or sporting gear. Those transactions are completed with the external provider and concern real-world services or physical goods; they do not unlock content or functionality in Rallii. Some links are affiliate links, as disclosed in the app, and Rallii may receive a commission.

3. What specific types of previously purchased content, subscriptions, features, and services can a user access in the app?

None. Rallii does not provide access to previously purchased digital content, subscriptions, or paid features. No prior purchase, membership, or purchase restoration is required to use the available app functionality. External travel or retail purchases do not grant app access or app entitlements.

4. What paid content, subscriptions, or features are unlocked within the app that do not use In-App Purchase?

None. There are no paid digital unlocks, subscriptions, or premium features in this release, through In-App Purchase or any other mechanism. The previous Pro wording referred to planned functionality and has been removed. All functionality available in this release is free.
